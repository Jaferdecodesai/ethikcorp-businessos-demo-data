## 3. Services
### Homepage service lines
1. Business Transformation Consulting: from Lean & Six Sigma process optimisation to full-scale organisational redesign.
2. AI-Enabled Business Transformation: process redesign, decision support, operational intelligence.
3. AI Agents for Workflow Automation: lead qualification, approvals, routine queries, working around the clock with the client's existing systems.
4. Corporate Training Programs in Dubai: on-site, online or blended. Covers management, sales, customer service, HR, soft skills and compliance. Every program starts with a Training Needs Analysis (TNA) and ends with an evaluation report.
5. Leadership Development & Culture Transformation: from emerging managers to senior executives, with structured change management.
6. Gamification in Learning & Corporate Education: includes "a full gamified MBA module for a university in the UAE".

### Methodology (homepage)
Diagnose & discover → Design the solution → Deliver & embed → Measure & report (evaluation at 30, 60 and 90 days, KPI tracking, ROI reports).
The transformation page uses a different set of steps: Diagnose → Design → Activate → Execute → Sustain.

- 04 AI for Leadership & Employees: executive AI assistants, knowledge assistants, AI productivity systems, AI employee training, internal company AI, decision-support tools.

### Corporate Training page (/corporate-training-solutions/)
- Programs: Management Training; Leadership Development; Team Building; Business & Sales; Soft Skills & Communication; Online Corporate Training; Customer Service; HR & Employee Development.
- Process: Discovery & Needs Analysis → Program Design → Training Delivery → Evaluation & Reporting.
- Differentiators: TNA first; UAE-specific content; certified, accredited trainers; flexible formats and scheduling; post-training evaluation reports; corporate group packages; complete training management.

### Games Revolution (division) (/games-revolution/)
"Where technology meets creativity". Offers custom gamification solutions, VR/AR gaming, metaverse development, and cross-platform game creation (mobile, desktop, console).

"Free BizScan · Business Health Check": a free business scan for owners, CEOs and leadership teams. The blog post on it lists 7 pressure points: slower decisions, delayed projects, pipeline pressure, rising costs, cash strain, internal confusion, leadership overload. CTA via WhatsApp.
