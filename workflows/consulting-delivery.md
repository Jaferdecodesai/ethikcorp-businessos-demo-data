# Consulting Delivery — current manual workflows

> SAMPLE DATA for demo – not real Ethikcorp figures
>
> Snapshot used for Businessbot automation analysis. Time estimates are illustrative weekly hours for a 6-person team (960 hours available in Oct 2026, 816 utilised, 768 billable). This is the firm’s main delivery engine.

## Purpose
Diagnose, design, deliver and measure transformation, AI and leadership engagements. Public methodology: Diagnose & discover → Design the solution → Deliver & embed → Measure & report, with evaluations at 30, 60 and 90 days. Training interventions typically run 2–4 days; full transformation runs 3–12 months.

## Workflow 1 — Mobilisation and kickoff (6 hours / person / week, front-loaded on new work)
When Sales marks a deal won, Consulting Delivery is told by email or chat. A client partner builds a kickoff pack (scope, team, timeline, risks) from a previous deck, retyping commercial terms from the PDF proposal.

**Friction:** scope and rates sometimes drift from the signed proposal. Mobilisation waits until someone forwards the file. There is no standard engagement workspace opened on Closed Won.

## Workflow 2 — Weekly delivery control (10 hours / person / week)
Progress lives in slide decks, email status notes and a shared timesheet spreadsheet. Risks, decisions and actions are copied between those three places. Capacity is planned on a whiteboard or in a spreadsheet that is updated on Sundays.

**Steps**
1. Consultants update timesheets (often late).
2. The client partner compiles a status slide.
3. Internal review, then a client weekly call.
4. Actions are written in meeting notes and re-typed into an email.

**Friction:** late timesheets hide under-utilisation and overruns. Issues surface in the weekly call instead of when they occur. Leadership cannot see delivery risk across all active engagements in one view.

## Workflow 3 — 30 / 60 / 90-day measurement (4 hours / person / week, peaked around review dates)
Evaluation reports are written in Word from interview notes and whatever KPIs the client can export. The same report is later re-used by Client Success and by Finance when a value-based conversation is needed.

**Friction:** KPI definitions are not stored at kickoff, so each review starts from scratch. Missed review dates are common when the date lives only in a personal calendar.

## Time that is a candidate for automation
- Open an engagement workspace, kickoff checklist and staffing plan automatically from the won proposal.
- Capture time, risks and actions once, and generate the weekly status pack from that record.
- Hold the 30/60/90-day review dates and KPI definitions in the engagement record, and draft the evaluation report from them.
- A live utilisation and delivery-risk board for all active work.

## Expected effect if automated (illustrative, not a commitment)
Reducing coordination time even modestly on a 6-person, highly utilised team returns hours to billable client work and protects margin on fixed-price transformation engagements. Fewer missed 90-day reviews also supports the retention and expansion motion owned with Client Success.
