# Sales & Business Development — current manual workflows

> SAMPLE DATA for demo – not real Ethikcorp figures
>
> Snapshot used for Businessbot automation analysis. Time estimates are illustrative weekly hours for a 3-person team (480 hours available in Oct 2026, 360 utilised). They are not recorded timesheets.

## Purpose
Win new Ethikcorp work and expand existing accounts. The public offer starts with a free BizScan business health check, then a consultation, then a scoped proposal. Pricing is quoted per engagement; there is no public price list.

## Workflow 1 — Lead intake and BizScan (8 hours / person / week)
Today, inbound interest arrives through WhatsApp (+971 public company number on the website), the website contact form, and LinkedIn. A business developer copies each enquiry into a shared spreadsheet, sends a standard BizScan questionnaire as a WhatsApp message or email, and books a scan call in a personal calendar.

**Steps**
1. Notice a WhatsApp, form or LinkedIn message (often hours later).
2. Create or update a row in the leads spreadsheet (name, company, sector, source).
3. Send the BizScan questions and wait for a reply.
4. Hold a 45–60 minute scan call and type notes into a document.
5. Email a short findings note and offer a consultation.

**Friction:** leads sit in personal inboxes; there is no single pipeline view; follow-up depends on memory. Duplicate companies appear when two people reply to the same thread.

## Workflow 2 — Qualification, consultation and proposal (12 hours / person / week)
Qualified prospects get a consultation, often with a client partner from Consulting Delivery. The proposal is written in a Word template, costed in a separate spreadsheet, exported to PDF, and sent by email.

**Steps**
1. Qualify sector, budget range, decision maker and timing.
2. Book a consultation and circulate a briefing note by email.
3. Draft the proposal (scope, team, timeline, commercial).
4. Internal review by Head of Sales (and Legal for government or healthcare).
5. Send the PDF and log the version number in the spreadsheet.

**Friction:** version control lives in email. Pricing assumptions are re-typed for every deal. There is no automatic link from a won proposal to Finance (so invoicing starts late) or to Consulting Delivery (so mobilisation waits on a kickoff email).

## Workflow 3 — Pipeline review and follow-up (6 hours / person / week)
The Head of Sales runs a weekly pipeline meeting from the spreadsheet. Stages are updated by hand (Qualification, Discovery, Proposal, Negotiation, Closed Won, Closed Lost). Follow-ups are calendar reminders, not a cadence in a CRM.

**Friction:** stage ages, stale deals and forecast roll-ups are assembled the night before the meeting. Win/loss reasons are optional free text, so coaching patterns are hard to see.

## Time that is a candidate for automation
- Capture WhatsApp and form enquiries into one lead record, with no re-typing.
- Auto-send the BizScan questionnaire and book the scan call.
- Build a first-draft proposal from the approved template, service catalogue and rate card.
- Keep stage, value and next action current, and alert when a deal goes stale.
- On Closed Won, open a client record, a kickoff checklist and a first invoice request.

## Expected effect if automated (illustrative, not a commitment)
The team spends a large share of utilised time on coordination rather than selling. Recovering even 8–10 hours per person per week would increase time spent on live deals and should raise conversion of Proposal and Negotiation stages, which is the main revenue lever for this department.
