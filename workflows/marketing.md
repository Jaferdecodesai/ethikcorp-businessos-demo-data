# Marketing — current manual workflows

> SAMPLE DATA for demo – not real Ethikcorp figures
>
> Snapshot used for Businessbot automation analysis. Time estimates are illustrative weekly hours for a 2-person team (320 hours available in Oct 2026, 240 utilised).

## Purpose
Generate qualified demand for BizScan and consultations, and keep the public brand consistent: “Dubai’s People Led Transformation Partner.” Channels named on the public site are the website and blog, LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook, YouTube and WhatsApp.

## Workflow 1 — Content and blog production (10 hours / person / week)
Blog posts are drafted in Google Docs, reviewed by email, and pasted into WordPress by hand. There is no documented content calendar. The public site already has a set of thought-leadership posts (transformation, process optimisation, training ROI, BizScan), but publication dates and performance are not tracked in one place.

**Steps**
1. Agree a topic in a chat message or meeting.
2. Draft and circulate for review.
3. Apply comments, export, and paste into WordPress.
4. Add images and SEO fields manually.
5. Share the link on LinkedIn and Instagram, copying the caption each time.

**Friction:** reviews stall in inboxes. The same proof points (programme counts, satisfaction, retention) still differ from page to page on the public site, and updates are easy to miss when each page is edited separately.

## Workflow 2 — Campaign and lead-magnet handling (6 hours / person / week)
The primary lead magnet is the free BizScan. Campaign ideas (LinkedIn posts, WhatsApp nudges, event follow-ups) sit in a shared note. Responses are forwarded to Sales by WhatsApp or email, not written into a lead record automatically.

**Friction:** Marketing cannot see which campaigns produced consultations or won work. Sales cannot see the original campaign source on the lead.

## Workflow 3 — Brand, asset and proof-point control (4 hours / person / week)
Decks, one-pagers and “Trusted By” logo use are stored in shared drives with informal naming. The public knowledge base already flags that logo permissions and site statistics should be verified before external use. Those checks are done ad hoc when someone is about to publish.

**Friction:** teams reuse out-of-date statistics. There is no single approved claims list, and no record of which client logos have written permission.

## Time that is a candidate for automation
- A simple content calendar with draft, review, publish and social syndication steps.
- A single approved claims and logo-permission register, used by the website, decks and proposals.
- Automatic hand-off of BizScan and campaign replies into the Sales lead record, with source tagged.
- A monthly performance digest (traffic, enquiries, consultations) without spreadsheet assembly.

## Expected effect if automated (illustrative, not a commitment)
Faster, consistent publishing and a clean Marketing-to-Sales hand-off should increase qualified BizScan volume. A claims register reduces the risk of publishing unverified logos or conflicting statistics, which protects revenue as well as reputation.
