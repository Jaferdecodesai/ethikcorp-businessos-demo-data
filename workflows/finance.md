# Finance — current manual workflows

> SAMPLE DATA for demo – not real Ethikcorp figures
>
> Snapshot used for Businessbot automation analysis. Time estimates are illustrative weekly hours for a 2-person team (320 hours available in Oct 2026, 224 utilised). All hours in this department are non-billable.

## Purpose
Quote support, invoicing, collections, cash visibility and simple management reporting in AED. The public site does not list prices; fees are quoted per engagement based on participants, duration, customisation and format.

## Workflow 1 — Billing from signed work (8 hours / person / week)
Invoices are raised from a copy of the signed proposal or an email from Sales. A spreadsheet lists invoice number, client, amount, VAT, due date and status. Each invoice is typed into the accounting tool, exported to PDF, and emailed.

**Friction:** there is no automatic Closed Won trigger, so the first invoice can lag mobilisation. Milestone bills (for 3–12 month transformation work) depend on someone telling Finance that a milestone was met. Credit notes and variations are easy to miss.

## Workflow 2 — Collections and cash (5 hours / person / week)
Overdue items are found by filtering the spreadsheet. Chasers are personal emails. Cash-in for the week is assembled every Sunday for the CEO.

**Friction:** no ageing dashboard, no standard chase cadence, and no link to Client Success when a large invoice is overdue (which may signal an account issue).

## Workflow 3 — Management reporting (5 hours / person / week)
Revenue by service line, utilisation and pipeline coverage are combined by hand from the timesheet file, the invoice file and the Sales spreadsheet. Figures are pasted into a slide for the weekly leadership review.

**Friction:** the three sources disagree when a timesheet or invoice is late. The CEO does not have a live view of recognised revenue versus bookings versus cash.

## Time that is a candidate for automation
- Create the invoice schedule from the won proposal (milestones, training dates, VAT).
- Raise and send invoices on the scheduled date, and post them to the ledger.
- Automatic chase cadence and an ageing view, with a note to Client Success on overdue balances above a threshold.
- A live AED pack: recognised revenue, bookings, cash collected, and forecast — the same figures the BusinessOS dashboards should chart.

## Expected effect if automated (illustrative, not a commitment)
Faster billing and a tighter collection cycle improve cash. Clean, consistent figures let leadership act on margin and forecast issues in the week they appear, rather than after a month-end tidy-up.
