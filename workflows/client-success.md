# Client Success — current manual workflows

> SAMPLE DATA for demo – not real Ethikcorp figures
>
> Snapshot used for Businessbot automation analysis. Time estimates are illustrative weekly hours for a 3-person team (480 hours available in Oct 2026, 384 utilised, 240 billable).

## Purpose
Keep the promise of long-term partnership: track agreed KPIs at 30, 60 and 90 days, issue a formal ROI / evaluation report, watch account health, and find expansion opportunities. Public claims (to be treated as marketing, not measured facts) include high retention and satisfaction; this sample does not invent those as actual scores.

## Workflow 1 — Onboarding and success plan (5 hours / person / week)
After kickoff, Client Success copies scope and promised outcomes from the proposal into a success-plan document. Contacts, review dates and risks are typed again into a spreadsheet.

**Friction:** if mobilisation is late or the proposal file is not forwarded, the success plan starts after the first delivery week. Contacts are often incomplete.

## Workflow 2 — 30 / 60 / 90-day reviews (8 hours / person / week, peaked around dates)
Review dates live in personal calendars. Before each review the manager gathers interviews, attendance, and whatever KPI export the client can share, then writes the report in Word with Consulting Delivery.

**Friction:** missed dates are not flagged. Reports vary in structure, so the CEO cannot compare outcomes across accounts. Expansion conversations happen informally, if at all, after the 90-day report is sent.

## Workflow 3 — Health, renewal and expansion (5 hours / person / week)
Account health is a conversation in the weekly meeting, not a score. Renewal dates (where a training academy or retainer might continue) are not listed in one place. Overdue invoices, delivery risks and unused scope are not combined into one view.

**Friction:** at-risk accounts are noticed late. Expansion (for example adding AI agents after a transformation, or a second training cohort) is left to Sales memory.

## Time that is a candidate for automation
- Create the success plan and review calendar automatically from the won engagement.
- Remind, collect inputs, and draft the 30/60/90-day report in a standard format.
- An account-health view that includes delivery risk, overdue invoices, unused scope and next renewal date.
- A structured expansion suggestion to Sales when a 90-day review shows a clear next service line.

## Expected effect if automated (illustrative, not a commitment)
On-time reviews and a visible health score should improve renewal and expansion, which is the lowest-cost way to grow recognised revenue. The sample pipeline already shows several multi-service opportunities (transformation clients who have not yet bought training or agents, and the reverse).
