# Operations — current manual workflows

> SAMPLE DATA for demo – not real Ethikcorp figures
>
> Snapshot used for Businessbot automation analysis. Time estimates are illustrative weekly hours for a 3-person team (480 hours available in Oct 2026, 360 utilised, 48 billable for programme coordination).

## Purpose
Make delivery actually happen on the ground across the UAE (Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah and other emirates), including virtual and blended formats. This covers trainer and consultant scheduling, venues, travel, materials, and “complete training management” from TNA through to the 90-day follow-up logistics.

## Workflow 1 — Scheduling and resource booking (10 hours / person / week)
Training dates and on-site workshops are agreed in email, then copied into a shared calendar and a spreadsheet of who is where. Venue options are searched and booked by phone or web forms. Travel is booked in the same thread.

**Friction:** double-booking happens when a consultant agrees a client date in a chat that Operations has not seen. Changes (a postponed cohort, a second workshop) are easy to lose. The calendar and the spreadsheet drift apart.

## Workflow 2 — Materials, venues and day-of coordination (6 hours / person / week)
Workbooks, attendance sheets and feedback forms are printed or emailed the day before. Attendance is taken on paper or in a door-sheet and typed up later for the evaluation report and for Finance (if pricing depended on participant count).

**Friction:** attendance and feedback reach Consulting Delivery and Client Success late, which delays the evaluation report and any participant-based invoice.

## Workflow 3 — Supplier and logistics tracking (4 hours / person / week)
Venue, print and transport suppliers are paid from emailed invoices forwarded to Finance. There is no simple supplier list with rates, preferred venues by emirate, or on-time history.

## Time that is a candidate for automation
- A single schedule that Sales, Consulting Delivery, HR (availability and visas) and Operations all see, with conflict checks.
- Auto-generated attendance and feedback forms, with results written straight into the engagement record.
- A venue and supplier shortlist by emirate, with request and confirmation templates.
- Change requests (date moves, extra cohorts) that update calendar, travel and billing milestones together.

## Expected effect if automated (illustrative, not a commitment)
Fewer scheduling clashes and faster attendance capture protect billed training days and speed invoicing. Tight logistics are especially valuable in the Corporate Training and Leadership service lines, which have many short, date-bound events.
