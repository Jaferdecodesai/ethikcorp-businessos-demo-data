# HR & Talent — current manual workflows

> SAMPLE DATA for demo – not real Ethikcorp figures
>
> Snapshot used for Businessbot automation analysis. Time estimates are illustrative weekly hours for a 2-person team (320 hours available in Oct 2026, 208 utilised). No individual staff names or personal contact details are included in this sample.

## Purpose
Staff the nine departments, keep visas and labour-law records in order, and hold the credentials the brand promises (“certified, experienced practitioners”, accredited trainers, Dare to Lead and coaching certifications). The public site has no careers or team pages; hiring is mostly referral and LinkedIn outreach.

## Workflow 1 — Hiring and onboarding (8 hours / person / week, variable)
Roles are agreed in a meeting, posted or messaged by hand, and candidates are tracked in a spreadsheet. Offers are Word letters. Onboarding is a checklist in email: equipment, email account, introduction to the department head, and a first-week plan.

**Friction:** hiring status is not visible to the requesting department. New joiners start without a complete credentials file, which later blocks them from being staffed on a client that asked for proof of certification.

## Workflow 2 — Credentials, visas and contracts (6 hours / person / week)
Trainer and consultant certificates, labour contracts and visa dates live in personal or shared folders. Expiry dates are reviewed when someone thinks to open the folder.

**Friction:** a missed visa or certificate expiry can stop on-site delivery in the UAE and delay a billed training date. There is no automatic reminder to the consultant and the Operations scheduler.

## Workflow 3 — Capacity and people data for staffing (3 hours / person / week)
Consulting Delivery asks HR who is available next month. HR answers from memory, the timesheet file, and leave messages in chat.

**Friction:** staffing decisions are slower than they should be on a 26-person firm, and leave clashes are found after a client date is committed.

## Time that is a candidate for automation
- A light applicant tracker with a standard offer and onboarding checklist.
- A credentials and visa register with expiry alerts to HR, the person, and Operations.
- A weekly availability view (leave, utilisation, certifications) that Consulting Delivery can use for staffing.
- A reminder to collect credentials before a person is assigned to an engagement.

## Expected effect if automated (illustrative, not a commitment)
Fewer expired-document surprises protects billed delivery days. Faster staffing of the right certified person improves utilisation in Consulting Delivery and Technology & AI, which is the firm’s revenue engine. This sample does not include personal HR files and should not be used as a source of individual data.
