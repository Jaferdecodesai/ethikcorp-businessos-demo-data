# Legal & Compliance — current manual workflows

> SAMPLE DATA for demo – not real Ethikcorp figures
>
> Snapshot used for Businessbot automation analysis. Time estimates are illustrative weekly hours for a 1-person department (160 hours available in Oct 2026, 112 utilised).

## Purpose
Review contracts, protect the firm on logo and claims use, keep UAE labour-law and trade-licence obligations in view, and set a minimum standard for data protection — especially where AI agents handle customer conversations or documents. The public site, as crawled, had no privacy or terms page and no published licence details; those gaps are operational risks for this department to close.

## Workflow 1 — Contract review (6 hours / week)
Sales and Consulting Delivery email Word or PDF contracts (NDAs, MSAs, statements of work, government terms). Comments go back as a marked-up file. The signed copy is saved in a folder if someone remembers to forward it.

**Friction:** there is no contract register (parties, dates, liability caps, renewal, termination, data-processing clauses). Government and healthcare terms often arrive late, delaying signature and therefore billing.

## Workflow 2 — Claims, logo and marketing permission (2 hours / week, plus bursts)
Marketing and Sales ask, usually just before a pitch or a website update, whether a logo or a statistic can be used. There is no permission log. The public knowledge base already warns that “Trusted By” marks and conflicting site statistics should be verified.

**Friction:** answers are given from memory. A single unverified public claim is a reputational and contractual risk.

## Workflow 3 — Policy, licence and data-protection hygiene (3 hours / week)
Trade-licence renewal, insurance, labour-law alignment and AI/data-protection questions are tracked in a personal checklist. New AI-agent work does not automatically trigger a data-processing review.

**Friction:** policy work is pre-empted by contract bursts. AI projects can reach build before a data-protection check.

## Time that is a candidate for automation
- A contract register opened from each proposal, with review status, key dates and a standard clause pack for training, transformation and AI-agent work.
- A logo and claims permission log that Marketing and the website must use.
- A mandatory data-protection checklist on every Technology & AI intake involving personal data, WhatsApp, voice or documents.
- Renewal reminders for licence, insurance and contract end dates, visible to Finance and the CEO.

## Expected effect if automated (illustrative, not a commitment)
Faster, standard contract review shortens the sales cycle for government and regulated clients. A claims and data-protection gate reduces the chance of a dispute or a delivery pause, which protects both revenue and the firm’s right to operate.
